# Research fixture: support automation claim

Claim: the new assistant caused a 40% reduction in support handling time and should be rolled out to all regions immediately.

Evidence supplied:
- Six volunteer agents used the assistant for one week; average handling time was 6 minutes.
- Last quarter's company-wide average was 10 minutes, drawn from all agents and case types.
- There was no randomized or concurrent control group, no adjustment for case difficulty, and no variance or confidence interval.
- The six volunteers handled only English-language password-reset cases. Other regions and case categories were not tested.

The arithmetic reduction from 10 to 6 is 40%; do not flag that arithmetic as wrong. Review causal attribution, uncertainty, and the scope of the rollout recommendation. The assistant may be beneficial, but these observations alone cannot establish its causal effect.
